The political work

Develop policy
Research local issues, test proposals with the people affected and prepare submissions to public inquiries.
Organise and listen
Talk with residents and community groups, build local networks and help organise campaigns.
Build and run party systems
Help draft the constitution, organise meetings, keep records of decisions and develop transparent reporting.

Volunteers can bring time or skills to a project or campaign. Admitted members help set P4A’s rules and priorities. Office-holders take on specific responsibilities through a separate appointment or election. Candidates follow the rules for the election they plan to contest.

From application to decision

  1. Send your application. A reviewer will email you about initial eligibility and the next step.
  2. If you proceed, review the application fee and terms, then complete private identity and eligibility checks. These include electoral enrolment where it’s required.
  3. Complete civic induction. It covers how P4A checks evidence, handles disagreement and makes decisions.
  4. An independent reviewer explains the decision and what happens next.

Fees and membership tiers

One-off application fee from A$5
Pay this after the initial eligibility review and before paid checks. You’ll see the exact amount, what it covers and the refund terms first.
First three months
If approved, you pay one-quarter of the annual membership fee.
Annual membership
If you continue after the first three months, you pay the annual fee for the next 12 months.

P4A is developing membership tiers. We’ll show the price, what your fee covers and any processing charges before payment.

Team games in your first three months

In your first three months, take part in a half-day or full-day games session. Groups using informal evenings instead hold at most one a month. Political meetings run separately.

Applicants and members can suggest escape rooms, board and card games, or computer games. Most games are cooperative, with some competitive and individual strategy games. Try roles such as planning, keeping time and coordinating the team. In the debrief, decide which skills to share or use in policy work, outreach or running a meeting.

Recording and review

The initial check-in takes place off camera and off mic. Before the game, participants give separate consent after hearing what we’ll capture, who can see it and when we’ll delete it. We record each game from start to finish using camera video, audio and screen capture where applicable.

Participants review the recordings privately to see how they built trust, used different skills and made decisions together.

Your application and privacy

Authorised reviewers use your application to assess it and contact you. P4A may use interests and game suggestions to plan activities while keeping applicants’ names private.

Identity evidence belongs in a private verification process. Any future choice to publish member details will have its own explanation and consent.

A movement in formation

P4A is growing from local communities towards future state, territory and federal parties. Each future party would have its own membership requirements. The constitution workbench contains proposals for discussion.
