From application to decision

  1. A reviewer reads your application and emails you about initial eligibility and next steps.
  2. If you proceed, you’ll see the application fee and terms before paying. You’ll then complete private checks for identity and eligibility, including enrolment where needed.
  3. Civic induction covers how P4A works, including checking evidence, disagreeing respectfully and sharing responsibility for decisions.
  4. An independent reviewer explains the admission decision and next steps.

Fees and membership tiers

One-off application fee from A$5
After the initial eligibility review and before paid checks, you’ll see the exact total, what it covers and the refund terms.
First three months
If approved, you pay one-quarter of the annual membership fee.
Annual membership
If you continue after the first three months, you pay the annual fee for the next 12 months.

P4A is developing membership tiers and will show prices and inclusions before payment. Cost-plus fees cover hosting, storage, software and included AI, with any payment processing charges itemised.

Ways to take part

Volunteer
Offer time or skills to a local project, working group or campaign activity.
Member
Complete admission and help shape P4A’s rules and priorities through shared civic work.
Office-holder
Take on a defined responsibility through a separate appointment or election, with checks suited to that role. Standing for public office follows the relevant election rules.

Teamwork in your first three months

Teams rotate through escape rooms, board and card games, and computer games suggested by applicants and members. Most are co-operative, with some competitive and individual strategy games. The debrief helps teams carry what they learn into civic work.

Recording selected moments

After the first check-in, P4A plans to use video, audio and screen capture for selected moments during games. The team reviews those moments privately to understand how people work together and learn from each other.

Before recording starts, P4A explains what it will capture, who can access it and how long it will be kept. Participants give separate consent for that session.

Your application and privacy

Authorised reviewers use your application to assess it and contact you. P4A may use interests and game suggestions to plan activities while keeping applicants’ names private.

Identity evidence belongs in a private verification process. Any future choice to publish member details will have its own explanation and consent.

A movement in formation

P4A is growing from local communities towards future state, territory and federal parties. Each future party would have its own membership requirements. The constitution workbench contains proposals for discussion.
