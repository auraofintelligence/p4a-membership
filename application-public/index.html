Review, checks and fees

  1. Initial eligibility

    A P4A reviewer reads your application and contacts you by email about the next step.

  2. One-off application fee from A$5

    After the initial eligibility check, you pay this fee before any paid identity or assessment checks. You’ll see the exact total, what it covers and the refund terms before you pay.

  3. Checks and induction

    Complete private identity and eligibility checks and learn how members take part in P4A.

  4. Admission decision

    An independent reviewer explains the decision and next steps.

  5. First three months

    If approved, you pay one-quarter of the annual membership fee for your first three months.

  6. Annual membership

    If you continue after the first three months, you pay the annual fee for the next 12 months.

Membership fees are cost-plus and cover hosting, storage, software and included AI. P4A itemises any payment processing charges before you pay.

Teamwork in your first three months

Joyful Responsible Abundance in action

Teams rotate through escape rooms, board and card games, and computer games. Most games are co-operative. The rotation also includes competitive and individual strategy games, with titles suggested by applicants and members. Teams build trust, draw on different skills and discuss what they can apply to their civic work.

Recording selected moments

After the first check-in, we’ll use video, audio and screen capture to record selected moments during games. We’ll review those moments together to understand how the team works, which roles suit different people, and how members can teach each other what they do well.

Before recording starts, P4A will explain what it records, who can access it and how long it keeps it. Participants give separate consent for that session before any recording begins.

How P4A uses your details

P4A, the movement operating this form, keeps your application in private Cloudflare storage. Authorised reviewers use your details to review your application and contact you. P4A can use interests and suggested games to plan activities and team sessions while keeping applicants’ names private.
