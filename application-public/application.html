Review, checks and fees

  1. Initial eligibility

    A reviewer reads your application and emails you about the next step.

  2. One-off application fee from A$5

    Pay after initial eligibility and before any paid checks. P4A shows the exact amount, what it covers and the refund terms first.

  3. Checks and decision

    Complete private identity and eligibility checks and civic induction. An independent reviewer explains the admission decision.

  4. First three months

    If approved, pay one-quarter of the annual membership fee.

  5. Annual membership

    If you continue, pay the annual fee for the next 12 months.

We show what your membership fee covers and any processing charges before you pay.
