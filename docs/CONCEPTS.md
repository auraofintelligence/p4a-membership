# Civic purpose and connected projects

Prepared 7 November 2026, Australia/Brisbane. Source review used the local project editions listed below. This application is the custom CRM and membership workspace for P4A the Purple Party for Australia. The existing public projects remain separately hosted.

The intended progression connects people, local groups and practical work with councils and electorates, then separately governed state and federal organisations. P4A is currently a movement in formation, has no adopted constitution and is not a registered political party. A website account, working-group participation, a paid service and organisational membership are separate relationships.

## Joyful Responsible Abundance

[Joyful Responsible Abundance](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/joyful-responsible-abundance/index.html) supplies the purpose for evaluating work: people gaining useful choices, capacity and authority over their lives. Its three questions can make a project's importance understandable:

| Lens | Project assessment prompt |
| --- | --- |
| Joy | Whose life improves, and how? Consider friendship, learning, rest, creativity and meaningful work. |
| Responsibility | Who decides, who carries the costs, and who can withhold consent? |
| Abundance | What useful time, care, knowledge, tools or capacity becomes more accessible? |

The CRM's separate urgency and importance values, each from 1 to 5, are a proposed human assessment method. They are not an established formula or validated instrument from the source. Keep the reason, evidence URL and review date with each assessment. Urgency concerns current harm, a real decision window and the consequences of delay. Importance concerns the desired change and people affected. Readiness and missing dependencies belong in the project's work plan, so an important project remains visible while people assemble what it needs.

The sovereignty paper's evidence cutoff is 5 November 2026. Its [five horizons](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/joyful-responsible-abundance/sovereignty-five-horizons.html), covering 2027, 2028, 2029, 2031 and 2036, are conditional scenarios with explicit assumptions and no assigned probabilities. They provide reasons to examine dependence, ownership and delivery evidence; they are not fixed deadlines or predicted outcomes.

## Installing the Missing Middle

[Installing the Missing Middle](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/installing-the-missing-middle/) proposes community compute, useful heat and local ownership through a federation of people and corporate vehicles. Installation, operations, learning, maintenance, acquisition and renewal are connected responsibilities. All postcodes enter discovery; actual installations follow participating people, facilities, workloads and agreements.

A `missing-middle` project in this CRM is a shared infrastructure opportunity. Members can join its working group, participate in the project, assign the next practical work through task records, discuss findings and review urgency and importance. The project's evidence link points to the relevant source or candidate brief. These are collaborative planning records, not claims of installed infrastructure.

Use a project work plan to establish:

- The place, proposed host and property owner, with their actual agreement status.
- Participating organisations, intended users and useful workloads.
- Power, connectivity, heat demand, physical access and specialist assessment needs.
- Delivery and operating responsibilities, finance assumptions, ownership pathway and equipment renewal.
- Learning capture, local skills and the owner of each unanswered question.

The current general project schema holds these details in the summary, tasks and discussion. Structured facility, workload and agreement records can extend it as opportunities develop. P4A membership, Ready SET Co-op membership and commercial participation must remain distinct. The source does not impose a compulsory corporate parent or require every participant to become a co-operative.

The source's 3,000-node baseline, future population of 30 million and approximately A$18–19 billion are planning scenarios. They are not operating capacity, funding awards or supplier quotations. Publicly named contributors are not automatically CRM users, appointed officers or authorised contacts.

## Verse Box

[Verse Box](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-verse-box/) proposes a portable, assisted introduction to personal Aura use, learning, creativity and civic participation. Its [trial](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-verse-box/trial.html) has no agreed hosts, dates, venues or tariffs. Everyday Aura use and civic participation do not require the separate optional Genesis programme.

A `verse-box` project provides a place to develop an assisted participation activity. Its task list can track the host agreement, facilitator, reusable equipment, accessibility, sample demonstration, permissions, take-home handover and clearing shared equipment. Discussion records can capture what participants want to explore. Proposed activities remain visibly proposed until their actual arrangements support a change of status. The CRM does not create public bookings or invent a trial schedule.

The intended participant journey is: choose an everyday task, try sample material, inspect and correct a result, choose what to keep or share, take a usable copy, and decide whether to continue. A project can follow a consented civic question and the resulting action. Personal memories, health records and private Aura archives remain under the person's own permissions, outside routine CRM collection.

Storage, interpretation, model training, external action and publication are separate permissions in the source. A general contact checkbox does not grant all of them. Future integrations must show the exact selected information and destination before transfer.

## Connections across the wider work

[Redlands Civic Lab](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/index.html), added at Luke's request on 11 November 2026, is the former mayoral campaign site reframed as a civic simulation. Luke is not contesting the 2026 Redland City mayoral by-election. The site explores Redlands questions, projects and possibilities, drawing on the local work behind Strange But True. It is linked from Connected ideas and the related content on Local nodes; this does not create a CRM group, political membership or an active campaign. Source: the Redlands Civic Lab README and Luke's clarification.

Your Relevance can help a person choose what matters and identify relevant places. Household Bill Check and the Australian Legal Engine retain their free public and local routes. Their optional CRM handoff is a participant-reviewed summary or research packet, not automatic access to private cases. Committees and inquiries remain a separately maintained public project. Locally developed twins can connect through source, place, observation date, uncertainty and permission records.

Keep three accounts connected but distinct: what it cost, what people gave freely and what changed. One C-Hour recognises one verified hour of voluntary human contribution, counted once, without monetary conversion or financial entitlement. Project participation, contribution receipts, evidence, decisions and outcomes form a practical foundation for the broader sensorium while each community retains its own authority.

## Gamify Democracy

The user explicitly requires monetary and voluntary contribution leaderboards as a core part of Gamify Democracy. The Strange But True Community Ledger supplies a contribution-recognition example alongside separate voluntary C-Hours. The user clarified that iCi/SOL belongs to their personal Strange But True project, for which they have already minted a Solana token. P4A's asset and network choices are independent and must be assessed for Australian legal, accounting and member needs. The membership design must support contribution recognition across people, projects, local nodes and wider civic scales, with verification, chosen visibility and evidence of outcomes. Financial giving, paid work and voluntary hours remain distinguishable. See [GAMIFY-DEMOCRACY.md](GAMIFY-DEMOCRACY.md) for the source mapping and implementation requirements. P4A's local build now implements the two leaderboards and manually reviewed contribution receipts. Live payments, cross-node credentials and blockchain integration remain outstanding.
