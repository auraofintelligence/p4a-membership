# P4A membership onboarding

P4A the Purple Party for Australia is a movement in formation. The workbench contains discussion drafts for a future constitution. The Civic training library is open to exploration and the membership pathway has its own induction and review.

## Walk through the rehearsal

Open **Membership onboarding** (`#onboarding`). Choose **Member demo** to work through the induction scenarios and send a fictional application for review. Switch to **Organiser demo** to review readiness, then return to **Member demo** to see the outcome or withdraw. In the GitHub Pages demo, changes last for the open page. In the local demo, they persist on the computer. Use fictional details in both.

The Cloudflare pilot shows the pathway and its status. The existing email aliases still resolve to one organiser account, with no fictional applicant or reviewer in that workspace. Real applications, verification and payments are closed.

## Open a real applicant entrance

1. Link from public P4A and committees pages to the free demo and, when ready, the hosted application. Name the operator and the organisation being joined. Set who can apply, what rights come with admission and who decides reviews and appeals. Keep the public civic resources accessible.
2. Bind each applicant to a distinct verified sign-in identity. Give applicants access to their own application, messages and outcome. Give independent reviewers individually assigned access to the applications they need to review. Test account recovery, role changes and departure. Keep the existing organiser access separate from applicant provisioning.
3. Show the approved privacy notice before collecting personal details. Explain who operates the service, why it collects the information, who may receive it, what happens if someone declines, and how to ask for access, correction or make a complaint. Record separate affirmative consent for the sensitive application information, with the notice and terms versions and dates. The application asks for contact details, local area, declared electoral enrolment, participation interests, game suggestions and a declaration of accuracy. Confirm identity and any electoral evidence privately when a defined check needs it. [OAIC collection and consent](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-3-app-3-collection-of-solicited-personal-information) · [OAIC collection notices](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-5-app-5-notification-of-the-collection-of-personal-information)
4. Connect the intended private Google register and verify its permissions, retention, backup and restore. The interim Drive register is separate from the pilot’s D1 store. Record minimal identity outcomes in the membership register. Keep raw identity evidence out of the interim personal Drive and ordinary organiser access. [Google storage setup](WORKSPACE.md)

Australian parties use different membership processes. Queensland LNP and the NSW Nationals both ask about local branches and policy interests. Queensland Labor's form includes a pledge and payment details, with a separate committee decision. NSW Liberals ask for proof of identity in their online form. P4A's application uses an initial review, private checks and a separate decision. [Queensland LNP form](https://www.lnp.org.au/getmedia/3a8ccbdd-1628-4bd5-8c22-25cc4c2d6f06/LNP-Membership-Application-20250910.pdf) · [NSW Nationals form](https://www.nswnationals.org.au/join/) · [Queensland Labor form](https://queenslandlabor.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Membership-Application-Form-2026.pdf) · [Queensland Labor approval](https://queenslandlabor.org/new-member-success/) · [NSW Liberals form](https://online.nsw.liberal.org.au/join)

## Make and record the admission decision

Check control of the account, then separately verify the person and the named organisation’s eligibility requirements. Compare an approved identity-verification service with assisted or in-person checks using authorised evidence. Apply the same approved assurance standard, offer a way to resolve inconclusive checks, and record the method, policy version, verifier, date and outcome. Set evidence access and retention before opening the service. [National Identity Proofing Guidelines](https://www.ag.gov.au/national-security/publications/national-identity-proofing-guidelines) · [Identity and transparency design](IDENTITY-AND-TRANSPARENCY.md)

Have a person review the practical induction responses, record acceptance of the current admission and conduct terms, and require an independent decision with a reason and next action. Route appeals to another reviewer. A separate electoral-registration step records who supports the named party’s application and collects the evidence required by that jurisdiction. Count only qualifying members for that purpose. [AEC registration guide](https://www.aec.gov.au/Parties_and_Representatives/Party_Registration/guide/files/party-registration-guide.pdf)

## First-quarter team games

Mix new and existing members for escape rooms, board or card games, and computer games during the first paid quarter. Make most sessions cooperative and include some competitive games and individual strategy games. Use the games people name in their applications and suggestions from members to plan a rotation. In the debrief, ask what helped people build trust, solve problems and communicate under pressure. Agree on one practice to try in a different game, working group or campaign and revisit it at a later session.

After the first check-in, use video, audio and screen capture to record selected moments during games. Review those moments privately with the team to understand how people work together, identify roles that suit them and help them teach skills to one another. Before recording starts, tell people what the host will capture, why, who can view it, how long it will be kept and how to ask for access or removal. Obtain affirmative permission from everyone within capture range for that event, separately from membership and game preferences. Check venue or game platform permission and the recording rules for the relevant locations, including where remote participants join. For children and young people, seek their agreement and permission from a parent or guardian. [Queensland recording law, sections 43 and 45](https://www.legislation.qld.gov.au/view/whole/html/inforce/current/act-1971-050) · [NSW surveillance law, sections 7 and 11](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/whole/pdf/inforce/current/act-2007-064) · [eSafety child-safe guidance](https://www.esafety.gov.au/communities/child-safe-communities/protection)

Use a private room or game lobby. Confirm who a shared microphone or camera can pick up, and keep bystanders, breaks and informal or private conversations out of the recording. Pause when someone joins, moves into range or withdraws permission. Limit access to the named session participants, keep the file out of the membership register, and delete it when the stated retention period ends, subject to any applicable preservation obligation. Share the learning outside the session as a de-identified summary. Obtain separate permission for any identifiable reuse. [OAIC information security and retention](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information) · [eSafety consent to sharing](https://www.esafety.gov.au/young-people/consent-sharing-photos-videos)

This build adds intake preferences and the operating guide. Capture, recording storage, replay access and deletion tools are future work.

## Set fees and public disclosure before collection

- **Application and membership.** Show the sequence on the application. After the initial eligibility review, a one-off assessment fee of at least A$5 covers application costs. Approval opens the first paid three months at one quarter of the annual fee. At the end of those three months, members who continue pay the annual fee for the next 12 months. Cost-plus membership covers hosting, storage, software and included AI upgrades, with any processing charge and agreed margin or reserve visible. Confirm the exact amounts, provider, fee classification, refunds and renewal terms before enabling payment. [AEC disclosure changes](https://www.aec.gov.au/FAQs/disclosure-legislation-changes.htm) · [ACCC price displays](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/pricing/price-displays)
- **Publication.** The two options for evaluation are a named money ledger with a separate membership-register choice, and a named ledger with a public membership register. Use the same admission standard. Show exact public fields before an affirmative choice, with a route to correct or withdraw voluntary disclosures. Public name, membership and verification status can be paired with a genuine zero-knowledge proof once supported. Keep identity evidence and payment credentials private. [Disclosure and proof design](IDENTITY-AND-TRANSPARENCY.md#member-controlled-public-disclosure)

## Test before opening

Run `npm test` and `npm run build:pages`. Rehearse applicant submission, review and withdrawal in a browser using keyboard and mobile layouts. In the hosted test environment, check that signed-out users and unrelated applicants cannot see application records, applicants cannot approve themselves, and retries do not duplicate records. Test incomplete identity checks, correction, independent appeal, consent withdrawal and Google recovery using fictional data before accepting real records. When payment and publication are added, test refunds, private records and the exact public projection before activation.
