# P4A membership: onboarding, committee, constitution and permissions matrix

**Purpose:** connect the three core P4A sites, two local entry points and two distinct regional/nation-led research resources into a clear onboarding journey and develop a real role-based membership and committee workspace using proven PLFC clubhouse patterns. Retain P4A's existing civic learning, group/project collaboration, Gamify Democracy, constitution drafts and private pilot. This is a development and acceptance specification, not authority to activate enrolment, appoint officers or adopt rules.

Reviewed 25 November 2026. Reference snapshots: [P4A membership](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/p4a-membership/tree/c013132fdbe9c5d2f81295203b2cfa60d34a2d47), [civic workbench](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/p4a-xyz-cinema/tree/d7d1ab52a9831ebc9c1158f0e26fe8531434e979), [committees and inquiries](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/p4a-xyz-committees-inquiries/tree/eec6a96860050869fbea3e9d840481ba64d6b5b9), [PLFC clubhouse](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/PLFC-members-club/tree/f5424be998cd3cb9b9e7e02817bb69be18e99e54), and [Sandy parity lessons](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Sandy-Sports-Club/blob/9359d2f21d05161cc0dd4d250fb6b8d03d9ac3dd/studio/PLFC-PARITY.md). ## Scope and principles

- P4A is a movement in formation, with no adopted constitution or party registration established by the reviewed source. Keep public civic resources, Household Bill Check, Legal Engine and inquiry discovery freely accessible; participation in public life never requires P4A membership.
- Existing public sites and related entry points are separate; do not combine databases, publish applicant data, transfer identities automatically, or regenerate unrelated national maps, electoral data or Twinkles just to add a link.
- Learn from PLFC's working accounts, committee desks, meetings, notices, role controls and isolated read-only showcase. Avoid Sandy's generic-record permission bypass and UI-only role preview. Adapt to civic governance rather than copying fishing, merchandise or club-specific rules.
- Keep original organiser profile, private pilot, application store and Workspace integration direction. Existing aliases for one organiser do not establish multiple independent reviewers. Do not recreate resources, reset credentials, send invites or activate real services as an incidental change.
- Preserve P4A's scoped L0/private and L1/shared design, independent ethical admission, genuine ZKP requirements, contribution separation, explicit disclosure choices, and cost-plus membership constraints in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md), [BUILD-CONTRACT.md](../BUILD-CONTRACT.md), [identity and transparency](IDENTITY-AND-TRANSPARENCY.md), and [Gamify Democracy](GAMIFY-DEMOCRACY.md).
- No automatic political entitlement from payment, voluntary time, training, game results, profile influences or account possession. No political profiling, coercive consent, invented people, fabricated votes or unapproved external contact.
- No em dashes in newly written or modified text. Use exact links, minimal scoped changes, cloud post-publication checks for the main civic site per its existing maintenance instructions, and honest distinction between tests, deployment and live acceptance.

## Proposed permission design

The table below is a proposed starting model, not enacted P4A rules. Office titles, election/appointment methods, grants, scope and authority require owner/member decisions. Roles are composable, scoped and time-bound, not a rigid hierarchy. Local groups do not inherit national access or automatically become political branches. Technical access is not political authority. Apply more restrictive rules for applicant identity evidence, financial evidence, complaints and private profile content.

| Role / capacity | Normal access | Mutations / decisions | Explicit restrictions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public visitor | Public resources, official inquiry links, public guide and separately labelled fictional demo | None in live private workspace | No membership required for public access; no applicant/CRM records |
| Applicant | Own verified application, messages, readiness and outcome | Own application, correction, withdrawal, approved consent choices | No other applicant or committee records, no member privileges by default |
| Member | Own records, consented profiles and entitled group/project resources, permitted constitution drafts | Own profile and contributions; proposals, participation and eligible votes only under applicable rules | No arbitrary membership approvals, role grants, raw identity/financial evidence or other private data |
| Committee member | Assigned committee, scope, permitted working records and duties | Propose/discuss, assigned tasks and eligible decisions | No unrelated committee/node or private applicant evidence by default |
| Chair / President (title to decide) | Scoped governance, meetings, resolutions and assigned review | Chair meeting, record authorised decision, delegate within agreed rule | No unilateral adoption, self-approval, technical escalation or broader scope by title |
| Deputy / Vice President | Scoped committee and delegated duties | Act within recorded temporary delegation | No automatic elevation or permanent chair rights |
| Secretary | Scoped agendas, minutes, notices, documents and assigned admission review | Prepare/review/release according to authority; record decisions and independent review | No unrestricted raw KYC evidence or self-review |
| Assistant Secretary | Explicitly delegated secretary tasks and permitted records | Agreed meeting, notice and document duties | No automatic full Secretary authority |
| Treasurer | Scoped budgets, financial evidence and receipt review | Reconcile, propose finance decisions, independently verify within limits | No self-verification, live transfer or unauthorised payment; C-Hours separate |
| Independent admission / evidence reviewer | Assigned cases and minimum necessary verification evidence | Reasoned review, clarification, decision or escalation under policy | No own case, unrelated records or public raw evidence |
| Real Webmaster | Authorised technical administration, permissions, service health, role previews, recovery and audit | Manage authorised accounts/grants, technical repairs, backup and restore | No substitution for independent political/governance decision; privileged data access logged and limited by purpose |
| Webmaster demo | Separate synthetic read-only full walkthrough and role examples | None in live or demo records | No production fallback, invitations, real exports, private applicant access or external actions |

Implement permissions as server-side action + resource + scope + actor checks, with distinct grants for view, create, edit, review, verify, approve, release, export, administer and delegate. Role names and UI labels are not sufficient. Check the policy on all alternate routes, projections, embedded views, histories, search, counts and exports. The real Webmaster's preview is not impersonation; demo is separate from the existing interactive fictional Pages experience.

## Status and priorities

P0 = release-blocking foundation, P1 = core functional development, P2 = deeper or externally dependent work. Gap = no comparable complete workflow found; Partial = useful code/design exists but essential work or acceptance remains; Substantial = significant existing functional implementation still needs integration/acceptance; Blocked = identified structural dependency; Hold = deliberate policy/activation hold. Accepted requires row-specific and common evidence; no row claimed accepted in this baseline. The priorities and role names are proposed, not owner-approved governance rules.

**53 acceptance units:** 17 P0, 33 P1, 3 P2. 1 blocked, 22 gaps, 27 partial, 2 substantial, 1 hold; 0 accepted. Not a percent-complete measure.
